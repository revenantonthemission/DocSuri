### Summary

The design is thorough. D8 and D9 are sound against `ControlLoop` and `OwnerTransactions`, the answers to Q1 to Q7 are followed, and R-12 to R-15 are closed in substance. Four Major gaps remain. The shared keyring read would reject U6's own rotated key file. Slow Notion reads can exhaust the shared request slots. A 401 can revoke the wrong connection. Preservation can delete keys before it is known to be safe.
