> R-01 Reviewer note: Workflow 4 step 3, the ExportEffect state-machine row, BR3.3 and BR4.6 now make accepted to executing and the first ExportAttempt one atomic compare-and-set. An executing effect always has an attempt, so the two recovery cases (outstanding attempt, elapsed rate-limit wait) cover every executing effect.

> R-02 Reviewer note: The state machine, BR4.4 and BR4.5 add `scanningStoppedAt`. A spent scan budget ends in owner settlement whatever the last scan result was. The state-machine row, the Workflow 6 trigger list and BR4.5 agree.

> R-03 Reviewer note: BR4.2 and Workflow 4 step 5 name exactly two retry signals: 429 `rate_limited` not marked blocked, and 529 `service_overload`. Everything else is uncertain and becomes unknown. The Notion facts section records the evidence (SDK retries exactly 429 and 529 for writes) and states that the rule is conservative.

> R-04 Reviewer note: A6 keeps `writable` as "may create here, as far as known" and adds separate `readable` and `creationCapability` fields. It records the reading of C09's "currently permitted destinations" as a deviation for approval at the U6 checkpoint. It maps rate-limited to `retry_later` with no committed disposition, adds a refusal class and the request id. The frozen outcome envelope already has `retryAfterSeconds`, so the wait is carried. The content and marker gap in the write and reconcile messages is raised separately below.

> R-05 Reviewer note: A4 adds `nextCheckAt` and `settlementOffered` to readEffect. A9 adds `invalidationReason` to the approval. The frontend data-source table maps every screen datum to a named frozen or amended field.

> R-06 Reviewer note: The entities and BR2.3 now carry a canonical block tree (`renderedBlocks`). `renderedContentHash` covers the tree, `renderedText` is its projection, and the marker is the last top-level block (BR4.1).

> R-07 Reviewer note: BR4.2 and Workflow 4 step 5 require the read-back page for a saved-but-busy answer to be under the destination, written by the attempt's writer, and to carry the marker as its last top-level block.

> R-08 Reviewer note: BR3.2 scopes idempotency keys per owner and operation. It states how an execute replay is recognised (the key, or the approval already having an effect) and what a reused key does on each call.

> R-09 Reviewer note: BR3.7 re-derives the binding on continue exactly as approve does. BR3.4 and Workflow 9 add the housekeeping invalidation of unconsumed approvals and the trimming of their rendered copy. Housekeeping on a source outage is raised below.

> R-10 Reviewer note: "이어서 내보내기" is offered only while the approving session is current. The outcome step states that signing out before the start stops the export. A session that has ended invalidates the approval (authority-revoked).

> R-11 Reviewer note: BR2.3 derives the source title from the result's `summary` (first sentence, prefix, fallback). I checked this against U4's built output shape in `backend/modules/research/evidence.py`: `summary`, `evidence`, `coverage` and `limitations` exist.

> R-12 Reviewer note: The export pair is defined once at the top of rules.md (owner, `artifactId`, `destinationId`) and used in BR2.5, BR3.1 and BR5.1.
