### Summary

The state machines stay inside C09's frozen transitions, and the press, cancel and settle paths guard well against duplicate or unauthorised Notion writes. The lifecycle design fits the participant pattern, and A8 follows the existing reason-key override precedent. Five Major gaps remain: a stuck-executing hole, an unknown-state dead end, an undefined "nothing processed" retry signal, a non-additive A6 change, and wire fields missing for the outcome step and the history. Together they make the design not yet buildable without guessing.
