### Summary

The rule set and traceability are sound (13 rules, full FR1 coverage, no orphans), but the state machine — this artifact's source of truth for transitions — contradicts its own rules on the unit's core safety property: the send guard omits the BR3.2 reconciliation pause, reconciled-sent runs skip the watermark, the empty-digest and discard paths have no terminal states, and the reconciliation evidence source is unmodeled, so a developer cannot implement delivery without guessing.
