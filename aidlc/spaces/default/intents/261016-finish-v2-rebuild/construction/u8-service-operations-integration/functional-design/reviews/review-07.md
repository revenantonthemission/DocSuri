### Summary

All five prior findings are fixed. The backup agent, its catalogue, the retention floor, the cleanup-marker check and the three rollback exits are modelled consistently across rules, entities and workflows. Three Major gaps remain. The profile's "current rehearsal database" has no writer, which blocks rehearsal database disposal and holds their backup sets indefinitely. The OS account that calls the agent is unnamed, and if it is the service user, the application can reach the agent's read-back. The write-time disposal date and record kind are caller-supplied and unchecked, which reopens early deletion of a newly written rollback copy. A developer could not build these three without guessing.
