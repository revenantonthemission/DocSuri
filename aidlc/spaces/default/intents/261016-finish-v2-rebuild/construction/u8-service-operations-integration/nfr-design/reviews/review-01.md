### Summary

The security design is thorough and mostly sound: the account, sudo, key, log-rotation and packet-filter controls trace to the requirements, and the open points R-20 and R-21 are addressed. Three design gaps would send an implementer to unsafe choices: the sign-in limit order that can let one address use up the shared bound, the missing rule for establishing HTTPS and Host behind the tunnel against the built route class, and the email-allowance reservation that adds a dependency cycle between the owner modules and ServiceOperations. With three Major findings the verdict is NOT-READY.
