### Summary

The requirement set is internally consistent. It traces completely and its numbers check out, and the earlier accepted log-rotation items are not reopened. Two Majors remain, within the two allowed. pgvector, which the application's own schema requires, is missing from the pinned and isolation-checked runtime set.
