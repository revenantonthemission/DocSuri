## Composition

A single solo stream: you (sole approver and maintainer) plus coding agents working serially from this session. No mobs are composed — the project is a solo-developer engagement, and the backlog is executed serially (IB-01 → IB-06, risk-first). [Q1] [intent-backlog.md]

## Why not a mob

Mob programming pays off when multiple people must share knowledge or parallelize; here the knowledge lives in the record, the capacity is one reviewer, and the approved execution model is serial construction with per-unit checkpoints. Composing mobs would add coordination without adding capacity. [Q1] [Q2]

## Working Agreement

- One work package at a time from the intent backlog; each ends in something that runs and merges through the CONVENTIONS.md PR flow.
- Agents self-organize implementation detail; you see evidence at gates, not intermediate process.
- Any failure or uncertainty stops at a gate with evidence and options — never silently worked around.
