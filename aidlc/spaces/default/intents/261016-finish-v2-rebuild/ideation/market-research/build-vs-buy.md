## Decision

Build on free/local stacks only. Paid SaaS, APIs and subscriptions are ineligible by policy; buy/partner options are excluded by policy rather than by evaluation, and no paid alternative was assessed. [Q4]

This restates the standing $0 budget rule recorded for this project ("The budget should be $0. Only use free or local stacks."), which the intent statement carries as a user-confirmed boundary. [intent-statement.md, Initial Scope Signal]

## Consequences

- No paid model APIs, paid discovery services, or hosted infrastructure enter the build; the finish work continues on the existing local stack. [Q4]
- Capabilities that competitors deliver through paid services must be met through local/free means or recorded as limitations — never silently substituted with a paid dependency. [Q4]
- Because licensing is tightening (see [market-trends.md](market-trends.md)), any integration that relies on free-tier terms needs its sustainability verified before the build depends on it. [Q3] [Q4]

## Assumptions & Open Questions

- Whether any current dependency relies on terms that could tighten or become paid is unverified and should be checked during the finish work's evidence stages. [assumption]
