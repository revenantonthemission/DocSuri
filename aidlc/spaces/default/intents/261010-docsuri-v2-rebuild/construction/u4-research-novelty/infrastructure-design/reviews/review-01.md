### Summary

The design is implementable and honest about its limits: every stage fails closed, refusals are never converted into skips, there is no paid path, PR jobs are secret-free, evidence is preserved before teardown, and migration/expiry/rollback make no destructive or erasure-completeness claim — no execution, deployment or numeric target is asserted. Two concerns remain: the C14 payload-enablement proof assumes a U4-local codec that the contract's fixed `AssistanceExecutionPayload`/PaperAssistance-only `readExecutionPayload` cannot return without an unnamed contract amendment, and traceability.json maps several requirements to targets that realize a different requirement. Both are correctable without redesign, so the verdict is READY.
