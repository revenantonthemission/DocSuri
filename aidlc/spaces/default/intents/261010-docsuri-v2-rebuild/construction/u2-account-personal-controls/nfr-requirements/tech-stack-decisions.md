## Primary guidance used

Accessed 2026-10-13. These sources guide investigation; provider capabilities, legal obligations and deployed configuration remain evidence tasks.

- [OWASP Password Storage](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Password_Storage_Cheat_Sheet.html) recommends memory-hard password protection and gives Argon2id configurations. Use it when choosing and measuring a supported configuration; this document does not approve a workload limit or infer an installed parameter set.
- [OWASP Session Management](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/cheatsheets/Session_Management_Cheat_Sheet.html) informs session lifecycle, cookie and transport review. Application requirements above additionally bind current authority and safe owner switching.
- [RFC 9700, OAuth 2.0 Security Best Current Practice](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9700.html) informs flow/callback protection, PKCE and downgrade handling. Determine actual provider support and client type before concrete adapter selection. Do not infer Google/ORCID deployment behavior merely from the standard.

No legal interpretation, current provider price/eligibility claim or vulnerability-clean claim is made here. Read official provider documentation and verify the actual configured account during G2; evaluate applicable privacy/content obligations before G3 choices.

## Measurement and verification

### Baseline procedure

For each scenario below, capture commit/build and lockfile identities, explicit synthetic environment identity, policy versions, seed/data cardinality, host/runtime allocation, test command, cold/warm state, concurrency and fault schedule. Recheck available resources before reserving any experiment; old hardware/free-disk observations are not allocations. Use only synthetic accounts and permitted fixture material until G3 permits real data.

Measure task completion with explicit denominator, useful/authoritative outcome assertions and separate rejected/failed/unknown counts. For durations report sample count, min/median/p95/max and raw safe samples; identify small samples where percentile estimates are unstable. Record UI feedback time, server/DB/queue/provider portions where observable, resource peaks, retry counts and recovery/diagnosis/repair time. Do not combine provider outages with local service latency or omit failed requests to improve numbers. Evaluate single actor, two conflicting actors/tabs, and a finite ascending load within an explicitly recorded safety envelope; stop on allocation/headroom thresholds. These are experiment requirements, not new public capacity promises.

After the reproducible baseline, propose numeric task success, responsiveness, recovery and repair targets with workload and cost/resource implications for human agreement. Correctness/security invariants remain pass/fail now; baseline-first does not make authority leaks, lost acknowledged writes or double accounting acceptable. Finite admission policy values must be set and verified before affected operation, even while service-quality objectives remain pending.

### Required finite policy register before operation

| Policy family | Required parameters and evidence (values still to be selected) | Failure behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Inputs and reads | Body/string/password/profile/query sizes; allowed vocabulary; page size/cursor expiry; response bounds | Reject before expensive work with safe validation; no silent truncation or unbounded scan. |
| Identity and proof | Session/continuation/challenge/enrollment expiry; attempts; rate windows; recovery/reauth rules; hash cost, concurrency and queue bound | Neutral initiation, protected rejection/backoff; no bypass when policy/store is unavailable. |
| Participants and accounting | Work admission, deadline/lease, retry/backoff, reconciliation batch/backlog, period/allocation/quantity and settlement/refund policy | Persist original responsibility, preserve accepted work, stop new admission at limits, expose blocked/unknown accurately. |
| Storage and retention | User/data/receipt/proof/log/cache/backup lifetimes and capacities; unresolved responsibility/tombstone preservation; cleanup and restore headroom | Stop unsafe growth/admission before losing mandatory evidence; pending obligations outlive optional telemetry eviction. |
| Public providers | Eligible account, terms, scopes, callback URLs, delivery allowance, timeouts and retry budget | Truthful unavailable/degraded capability; reconcile unknown outcomes; no paid overage or fallback. |

Each parameter needs owner, units/range, policy version, safe configuration validation, selected environment, evidence and change/rollback implications. G1–G8 in the security document assign closure. Settings for a synthetic experiment are explicitly isolated from user/product policy; public activation cannot inherit them silently.
