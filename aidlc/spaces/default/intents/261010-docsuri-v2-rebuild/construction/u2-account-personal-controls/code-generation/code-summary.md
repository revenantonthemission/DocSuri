## Implemented behavior

AccountAccess owns current sessions, credentials, purpose-bound proofs/continuations, identity fixtures, MFA material/enrollment, consent, profile and lifecycle. Registration/verification/login/logout, password reset/change, email replacement and full/limited continuations are mounted and tested; credential changes revoke sessions atomically and lost acknowledgements reconcile only the original receipt with the former cookie, original expiry/CSRF, identical intent and the current resulting authority. Google/ORCID adapters are explicitly labelled synthetic protocol fixtures with bound single-use flows, collision-safe linking and attributed optional profile data. MFA material has one protected local delivery, the QR renders locally, and TOTP accepts ±1 drift with strictly monotonic replay prevention and no administrator recovery bypass.

Library owns saved references/searches/history with immutable original acknowledgements, keyset pages, admission-sequence-fenced clears and source dispositions; viewing never reruns a query or writes history. Personalization owns explicit settings, acknowledged onboarding/interests (skip included), trusted optional activity under current owner/source/consent/input-boundary authority, bounded cold-start/manual fallback and reset responsibilities with a per-reset monotonic `statusRevision` separate from the settings revision; reset discovery is fresh and owner-bound. Usage extends the existing ledger with accepting-owner intents, bounded assignment periods/quantities, idempotent settlement and original-period reconciliation; a negative read, lease timeout or missing owner never releases responsibility. Lifecycle coordinates the installed 13-scope owner manifest with generation-fenced tickets, truthful pending/blocked/complete status, a narrow status cookie and quarantined restore enforcement; `ServiceOperations` scopes stay blocked without their evidence.

The Korean workspace keeps the inherited blue/neutral design, responsive components, appearance modes and keyboard/reflow behavior. Its Transport isolates late responses by authority generation and reconciles mutations explicitly; failed appearance persistence is marked unsaved rather than silently discarded.

## Failures found and repaired during final verification

- Capacity admission briefly gated static shell assets, which could leave a page unloadable while capacity was unobserved; the shell and in-memory catalogue are now exempt while every owner route stays admitted.
- A pending protected link was dropped by the bootstrap boundary remount and its completion notice was lost on authority refresh; both are retained now.
- The TOTP verifier rejected the documented next-window drift used by the approved journey.
- The identity return vocabulary rejected the installed `account` route.
- Fixture discards deleted MFA enrollments without their recovery rows, producing orphans that made every `pg_restore` fail; both discard helpers now remove recovery rows first and the realm is orphan-free.
- Test fixtures on the shared owner accumulated lifecycle requests and resets to the finite caps; affected suites now use dedicated run-scoped owners and reset cleanup, and the accumulated debris was reconciled.
- Flaky evidence was not accepted: failed runs, the SIGTERM selector defect, the restore ACL gap and the acquired-root-cause repairs are recorded in the check records rather than erased.
