### Validation

I cross-checked traceability.json mechanically against the rule IDs authored in rules.md: 35 authored rules, 34 distinct coverage targets, orphan rules = BR2.8 only, phantom targets = none. No other structural defects surfaced. The four entities in entities.md match the PlatformControl entity catalogue in components.md (PlatformEvidence, OperatorAuthority, ControlledOperation, OperationReceipt, each with no cross-component reference), and the workflows and rules align with contract C10's inspectEvidence/executeControlled/readReceipt semantics, the local stdin/stdout JSON envelope, the inventory-only vocabulary and the reconcile-without-duplicate-execution invariant. FR20.1 acceptance (distinguishable current/stale/mismatched/missing evidence, bounded authenticated reads, evidence not substituted for readiness) and FR20.2 acceptance (wrong actor/target, stale precondition and changed input rejected before effect; interrupted/duplicate outcomes recoverable; permission/disk/connectivity never reported completed; destructive/release needs explicit authority) are each covered by rules and workflows, and the design authorizes no real destructive or release execution (WF3 step 2, BR3.7).
