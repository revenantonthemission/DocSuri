### Summary

The revised design now delivers its central guarantee: a durable in-flight target lease is acquired in the pre-effect transaction, so two concurrent same-target `executeControlled` calls cannot both run an effect without holding a database transaction open across the child, and the child-to-parent settlement channel plus stale-`unknown` recovery are specified. It honours the answered decisions, invents no numeric targets or paid dependencies, and matches C10. The two remaining Major items are a wording conflict between security-design.md and reliability-design.md over what frees a crashed target's lease, and a stale traceability row (NFR8.1) that names a lock mechanism the design no longer uses; neither blocks implementation, but both should be corrected before the next stage reads them.
