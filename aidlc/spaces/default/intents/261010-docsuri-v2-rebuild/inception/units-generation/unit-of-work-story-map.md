## Assignment and acceptance semantics

All rows are **planned assignments; implementation and acceptance remain pending**. “Full-scope lead” means the unit accountable for closing the entire row with its listed contributors. It does not claim that the unit has implemented or tested the requirement. Parent rows close only after every subrequirement and applicable cross-boundary check closes. In particular, FR2 requires both U3 public content and U4 private content.

A unit may finish its bounded implementation contribution while the full cross-unit requirement remains open. For example, U2 verifies Library's changed-source contract against the existing host and controlled contract fixtures; U3 supplies the complete corpus-change implementation, and U8 verifies the combined behavior. That does not add a U2 → U3 construction prerequisite or permit U2 to claim full FR5.3 acceptance prematurely. Likewise, U2 completes lifecycle coordination with installed participants; later units verify their own participant duties before U8 can establish complete FR6.3 evidence. A fixture-only pass is not final integrated acceptance.

U1 owns a real, runnable find → understand → save → revisit increment with persistence, current owner checks, recoverable accepted work and essential feedback. Its participation below is a **partial contribution toward complete requirement parity**, not a mock and not an alternative reduced release. U1 must identify exactly which retained actions, fixtures and negative/recovery paths its demonstration exercises. Every remaining supported action, mode, provider and behavior stays assigned to the full-scope lead. The approved [P1/P2 tasks](../requirements-analysis/requirements.md#representative-public-paper-acceptance-tasks) supply the journey checks; they do not replace all-capability acceptance.

U8 supplies cumulative composition and acceptance evidence; it does not replace domain owners, take their private data authority, or defer every module integration check to a final stage. U2–U6 must run their embedded code and view contributions through an applicable real assembly with U1. Their `library` kind does not waive UI, security, durability, recovery, accessibility, observability or integration obligations. Other participating units below identify substantive completion contributions; they are not a replacement dependency DAG or a proposed scheduling order.
