# DocSuri v2 — External dependencies and evidence prerequisites

Date: 2026-10-11 (Asia/Seoul). Basis: the human-confirmed
[Delivery Planning summary](delivery-planning-questions.md#consolidated-summary-confirmation).
A **Bolt** is one build pass over a piece of work, ending in something that runs.
The confirmed order is B01/U1, B02/U7, B03/U2, B04/U3, B05/U4, B06/U5,
B07/U6 and B08/U8. Each unit remains serial and preserves the approved DAG.

## How to use this register

This register includes outside integrations and evidence needed before an
affected internal decision. It does not select providers, hosting, technologies,
migration policy, retention periods, quality targets or a release date.
References establish retained obligations, not present availability or agreement
by a provider. All lead times below are **unknown**, with no promised calendar
date; agents must measure or obtain evidence before relying on them.

For every entry, the human maintainer is the access/contact and product-decision
owner. Coding agents investigate and implement through the named component
owners. External parties control their own service terms and approvals; none is
claimed to be a committed project participant. Record primary-source retrieval
date, environment/configuration, permitted test identity and actual outcomes as
evidence arrives. Secrets belong in protected configuration, not this register.

Unknown evidence blocks its affected commitment or effect, while unrelated
analysis and safe isolated checks can continue. A mock, synthetic account,
local mail sink or degraded-state test must be labeled and cannot establish
normal-path public-service parity. Raise evidenced cost/capability conflicts to
the human without silent feature removal, paid fallback or new hardware.

## Closure evidence and slip or failure handling

### EXT-01 — Authority, continuity and recoverable data

- Identify the authoritative service/resources and inventory actual users, owner-linked
  saved/private data, artifacts and required key/configuration continuity. Record
  unknown access explicitly; do not infer an empty service from cleanup.
- Verify identified backup completeness and an isolated restore that preserves
  ownership, references, artifacts and permitted lifecycle state. The human then
  decides continuity/migration from evidence; source backups stay protected.
- If access or restore fails, preserve existing state and stop affected transition
  claims. Safe synthetic reconstruction may continue, labeled as such; it cannot
  substitute for the authoritative-user decision.

### EXT-02 — Sustainable $0 capacity

- Record available hardware/storage/network and permitted free-service limits,
  including overage prevention, expiry/trial conditions, model/download costs and
  operating burden. Exercise queue/upload/retry/cache/concurrency bounds and recovery.
- Require an actual permitted workload envelope for public registration and the
  affected capability. If fit fails, present measured alternatives within $0 and
  keep the affected feasibility condition open; no paid fallback or silent cuts.

### EXT-03 — Permitted processing and obligations

- Record content provenance, intended uses, permissions/terms, private-data classes,
  applicable location/institutional facts and required policy decisions using primary
  evidence. Obtain qualified clarification where evidence cannot settle applicability.
- If permission is unknown, withhold the affected processing/distribution and use
  separately permitted fixtures for limited engineering checks. Do not claim those
  fixtures establish permission for the production corpus or all private inputs.

### EXT-04 — Content and corpus sources

- Verify representative permitted metadata/content retrieval, formats, limits,
  canonical/version identity, source failure, withdrawal and rebuild inputs; prove
  coherent object/control/projection outcomes after interruption.
- On source failure, retain truthful availability and bounded retry/reconciliation.
  Previously eligible cached material follows current policy; unavailable/full-text
  gaps cannot become fabricated content or permanent loss of retained capability.

### EXT-05 — Retrieval, embeddings and citations

- Record compatible index/model/schema versions, source eligibility, citation
  provenance/limits and retained-mode relevance cases; exercise reindex, missing
  references, stale projections and withdrawal propagation.
- A disclosed supported degraded path may preserve use during outage, but semantic
  and citation normal paths still require evidence. Stop incompatible publication
  and repair/rebuild from authoritative inputs without erasing saved references.

### EXT-06 — Models and useful understanding

- Demonstrate actual permitted summary/translation and research/novelty behavior
  for documented inputs, with useful source-associated output and honest abstention;
  measure resource use, limits and extraction/model failure.
- If a free/local model cannot meet required outcomes, report the measured conflict
  before committing feasibility. Static answers, no-op workers or placeholder
  responses do not close this entry; preserve accepted-job recovery on outage.

### EXT-07 — Email and digests

- Verify authorized public verification/recovery delivery and actual permitted digest
  delivery under sustainable free/local limits. Exercise expired/used links, failed
  sends, queued unsubscribe/consent changes and timeout-after-send reconciliation.
- A local sink proves only local tests. Unknown remote outcome must stay unknown
  until reconciled; delayed setup keeps public-delivery readiness open, without
  silently removing verification, recovery or digests.

### EXT-08 — External identity

- Verify authorized Google/ORCID configuration and callbacks, intended local-account
  binding, collision/replay/denied-consent handling, unlink/revocation behavior and
  permitted ORCID profile suggestions with attributable source data.
- Provider outage may offer existing supported entry/manual onboarding behavior;
  it cannot silently create another account or count as successful retained linking.

### EXT-09 — Private inputs and web references

- Establish allowed text/PDF formats, size/complexity bounds, extraction/storage
  permissions and owner isolation. Test malformed/encrypted/truncated inputs,
  retained web references, evidence actually read and untrusted embedded instructions.
- Failed extraction or retrieval leaves a truthful recoverable state and no partial
  public publication. Permitted fixtures and failure tests cannot replace successful
  private-input and grounded-evidence normal paths.

### EXT-10 — Explicitly approved Notion effects

- Verify the retained write-only owner token configuration/read/disconnect behavior,
  accessible destinations, free limits and secret protection. This is not a new
  OAuth feature. Bind preview and approval to exact immutable source/destination.
- Use an authorized test destination and explicit effect approval; exercise revoked
  credentials, changed revision/destination, duplicates and timeout after remote
  success. Reconcile unknown outcomes before retry; no uncontrolled replacement export.

### EXT-11 — Lifecycle and acknowledged drafts

- Decide retention, deletion, reactivation, backup and shared-device treatment before
  affected implementation. Record policy revisions and each actual owner's lifecycle
  acknowledgement; distinguish revocation, erasure and externally retained copies.
- Close Contract R-02 with owner-scoped allocation/rediscovery and restart tests with
  no in-memory IDs before B05 draft persistence. If policy/evidence is unresolved,
  defer affected persistence choices; never promise recovery of unacknowledged text.

### EXT-12 — Consumers and compatibility

- Reconcile current routes, clients, provider modes, languages/levels, formats and
  operational sub-behaviors against the requirement ledger. Record actual consumers,
  or the evidence and limits behind an absence claim, before coordinated changes.
- Unknown consumers block unsupported compatibility claims. Preserve relied-on
  behavior until a supported transition is decided; do not add new public API scope
  or remove a retained feature merely because a source scan was shallow.

### EXT-13 — Controlled native operations

- Reconcile retained protocols and actual native filesystem/credential semantics;
  verify current actor/target authority, stale evidence, changed inputs, wrong targets,
  permissions/disk failures and interrupted/duplicate receipt reconciliation.
- Use safe isolated fixtures. Unsupported platforms remain an explicit gap; mocks
  do not prove native parity. Destructive/release effects require their specific
  operator authority; this register authorizes neither.

### EXT-14 — Measured quality and repairability

- Identify environment, workload, fixtures, sample size and raw observations for
  task completion, responsiveness, interruption recovery and defect reproduction/repair.
  Agree numeric service targets only after baseline; exercise the accessibility matrix.
- Record one-maintainer setup/diagnosis/repair and operating burden. Failed or
  unexecutable measurements stay visible. No invented dates, user validation,
  compliance certification or performance improvement closes the entry.

### EXT-15 — Cumulative verification and pipeline handoff

- Produce a real v2 unit-plus-cumulative application check and present its exact
  invocation for separate human approval before the first completion checkpoint
  runs verification. The command is unset now; deferral does not waive checks.
- Compare changed-work CI against the exact base under the interim policy. Carry
  reproducible v2 checks into the dedicated later CI/CD stages; inherited legacy
  failures, document validation and process liveness cannot certify application parity.

## Cross-cutting contract prerequisites

Contract R-01 requires browser-supplied revision conventions in B01 and complete
affected bindings in B03/later consumers. R-03 requires domain-owned worker claims,
payload retrieval and fencing in B01, extended in B04/B05. Their
[accepted review](../contract-design/reviews/review-01.md#findings) remains open
until executable closure evidence exists. B08 repeats the cumulative checks;
it is not the first point at which earlier units' required behavior works.
