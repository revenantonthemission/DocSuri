### Resolution Evidence

The prior finding descriptions/actions are retained above for continuity; the following current artifact evidence resolves them at design level.

- **R-01:** `functional-spec.md` > WF2, **T1-to-T2 recovery** (line 45), now expressly covers creator death after T1. Creator, explicit replay and the existing recovery actor compete for the same guarded T2 first claim; recovery scans include NOT_STARTED. - **R-02:** `functional-spec.md` > WF1 steps 3–5 (lines 34–36), reserves the revision before asynchronous work and checks owner/context/revision before either memory or storage publication. Synchronous publication has no intervening async yield; obsolete completions write nothing. Receipt metadata uses the same current-attempt persistence guard, and storage failure remains an honest memory-only outcome. `entities.md` > IdempotencyKey.preparationRevision and `rules.md` > BR1.5 agree with that ordering. In the former out-of-order A/B counterexample, A now fails its revision check before it can overwrite B.
- **R-03:** `functional-spec.md` > Legacy Compatibility Bridge (lines 59–72), distinguishes newly normalized legacy callers from pre-existing execution authority. New keyless calls receive separate authorityVersion 1 jobs and recorded dispatch identities without synthetic request merging; keyed legacy representation remains separate from the required browser digest. Terminal old messages are no-op acknowledgements, proven new-authority unversioned messages resolve existing dispatch identity after field validation, and active old work must be drained or isolated rather than receiving a fresh grant from missing ledger evidence. Isolation does not silently close an unresolved user outcome. `entities.md` > DurableJob.authorityVersion / SubmissionBinding.idempotencyKey and BR2.1 agree. Concrete environment procedures remain the C8 infrastructure/deployment handoff, not missing functional authority rules.
- **R-04:** `entities.md` > IdempotencyKey→TrackingContext (line 222), now explicitly permits tracking without recoverable attempt data. `functional-spec.md` > selects_over_time (line 179), uses optional-one cardinality at the attempt end and zero-to-many at the tracking end.
